Methodology

Scoring rules v0.1, updated 2026-10-16 (Decision 0010).

Each factor is scored 0–100 and shown separately, together with a weighted overall score.

FactorWeightHow it is judged
Completion rate30%Share of tasks completed as described, per public evidence.
Disputes20%Documented complaints, legal or regulatory actions and security advisories concerning the agent; fewer = higher score. Pending cases are recorded as allegations, not findings. Errors in the agent's output are scored under Completion rate.
Adherence to limits25%Stays within stated scope, permissions and safety limits.
Evidence quality25%Independent third-party sources rate high; vendor claims low; anonymous anecdotes lowest.

Rounding

The overall score is calculated from the unrounded factor weights and then rounded to the nearest whole number. A result of exactly .5 is rounded up (for example, 53.5 is shown as 54). Factor scores are whole numbers and are not rounded further.

Insufficient evidence

An overall score is given only when there are at least 3 independent performance sources. Otherwise the entry is labeled "Insufficient evidence" and no overall score is given; factor notes may still be shown.

Performance source: a source that tests or measures the agent's performance, behaviour or outcomes, such as a benchmark, research study, documented hands-on test, security or safety evaluation, or audit, and reports its method and results. Product descriptions, reviews, user-review aggregates, press coverage without original testing, commentary, court filings and vulnerability listings are recorded as other sources. They can inform factor scores but do not count toward the 3.

Independent: not produced, funded or co-authored by the vendor/maintainer or by a customer's staff, and not published by a competing product to promote itself. Studies by researchers who propose a competing research method may count; this is disclosed on the entry.

Counting: each study counts once, however many versions or links it has. A source counts only if it tested the product, version and scope covered by the entry. A source whose independence is only partial (for example, relying on data supplied by a customer) is labeled as such.

Evidence grade

The evidence grade describes the amount and type of independent evidence behind a score, not how well the agent performs. It is set by this table, using independent performance sources as defined above:

GradeIndependent performance sources
A7 or more, at least 3 peer-reviewed, at least 1 testing the current version within the last 12 months
B5–6, or 7+ without meeting the A conditions
B-3–4
Insufficient evidencefewer than 3 (no grade and no overall score)

The evidence-quality factor score should be consistent with the grade (as a guide: A ≥ 85, B 70–84, B- 55–69). If they diverge, the decision packet explains why.

Last reviewed

The date (America/New_York) an approved decision packet last re-checked an entry's sources and scores against the current rules. Wording, formatting or link-label fixes do not change it. A rules change that leaves an entry's outcome unchanged (e.g. Decision 0010) does not change it either. It is not a guarantee that information is current.

Corrections

If you believe an entry contains a factual error or misses a relevant public source, email trustkeeperace@gmail.com with the entry name, what you believe is wrong, and links to public sources.

  • Requests are reviewed under the same published rules as every entry. A request may or may not lead to a change; any change is made through an approved decision and listed in the changelog.
  • We do not promise a response or a response time.
  • Entries are not removed, added or re-scored for payment, and we do not accept payment for any listing or change.
  • Please do not send confidential or personal information. Only public sources can be used.
  • Sending a request does not create any agreement or obligation.
