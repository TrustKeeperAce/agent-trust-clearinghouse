How this directory works

An overall score is given only when at least three independent performance sources (tests by someone other than the vendor that report their method and results) have tested the product covered by the entry; otherwise the entry is labeled Insufficient evidence. Scored entries show the four factor scores separately next to the overall score, and every entry lists its cited sources. The full data is open under CC BY 4.0. Listing order is not a ranking, and nothing here is an endorsement or certification. More about how this directory works.

How scores work

Completion30%
Disputes20%

  • Four factors, shown separately: completion 30%, disputes 20%, adherence 25%, evidence 25%.
  • Fewer than 3 independent performance sources: no overall score.
  • Evidence grade describes the evidence, not the agent.
