Perplexity

Category: Verification · Type: commercial · Scope: citation-backed answering and source attribution only · Vendor site · Last reviewed: 2026-10-16

Summary

Perplexity is a commercial AI answer engine; this entry covers only its citation and source-attribution behavior. In independent evaluations (CJR Tow Center, 2025; Findings of EMNLP 2023; DeepResearch Bench, ICLR 2026; DeepResearchGym, ICTIR 2026; Deep Research Comparator, WWW 2026 Companion; Mind2Web 2, NeurIPS 2025), results varied by product mode: its Deep Research mode scored comparatively high on citation accuracy, while news-attribution tests found a share of incorrect or syndicated citations. Several publishers have filed copyright lawsuits against Perplexity that are pending; these are allegations, not findings. Informational only; not an endorsement or certification.

Legal disputes (filed; pending)

The following are allegations in pending lawsuits, recorded from court records. They are not findings.

  • Dow Jones & Co. and NYP Holdings filed suit (S.D.N.Y. 1:24-cv-07984), alleging copyright infringement, false designation of origin and trademark dilution. The court denied Perplexity's motion to dismiss or transfer on 2025-08-21; the case is pending.
  • Encyclopaedia Britannica and Merriam-Webster filed suit on 2025-09-10 (S.D.N.Y. 1:25-cv-07546), alleging copyright infringement; the case is pending.
  • Chicago Tribune Company filed suit on 2025-12-04, and The New York Times Company filed suit on 2025-12-05 (S.D.N.Y. 1:25-cv-10106), alleging copyright infringement; both are pending.
