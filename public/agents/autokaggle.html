Summary

AutoKaggle is an open-source research framework in which several AI agents work through a fixed sequence of data-science steps for tabular Kaggle competitions. Two independent studies that ran it were found, below the three needed for a score. In one, AutoKaggle produced valid outputs in 53-58% of trials across nine datasets, behind two other systems. In the other, its accuracy was close to other baselines. Its developers' own results are not counted. The code has not been updated since December 2024.
