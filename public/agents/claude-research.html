Summary

Claude Research is Anthropic's research mode in the Claude apps. It runs multiple connected web searches, and can also search connected tools, to produce cited reports. In independent 2025 tests, its report quality was mid-tier to strong (15th of 31 systems on DeepResearch Bench; 2nd of 9 for quality on DeepResearchEval), but it achieved 10% full success on hard Mind2Web 2 tasks, 60.7% of its statements were judged factually correct in DeepResearchEval, and in one dermatology test most references had fabricated authors or titles. Research now runs on Claude Opus 5.5 (September 2026), which has not been independently tested. Overall score 58. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Version note

Research launched in April 2025 and has been offered continuously under the same name, while its underlying model has changed. All counted tests were run in 2025 (Claude 3.7 / Opus 4 / Sonnet 4.5 era). According to Anthropic’s help page, Research now runs on Claude Opus 5.5 (released 22 September 2026), falling back to Claude Opus 4.8; this current configuration has not been independently tested. The tests are counted because the product is continuous (as in Decision 0019).

Connectors

Anthropic’s help page states that during research Claude can use tools from connected services “automatically, without asking for approval each time”, and that research started in Claude Mobile searches only the web, without any sign that connectors were skipped. These are vendor statements; this behaviour has not been independently tested.

Security and disputes

No lawsuit, regulatory action or security incident specific to Research was found. Security researchers have reported Claude-wide prompt-injection data exfiltration through Anthropic’s Files API; these are listed below as context and are not specific to Research.

Uncertainties

  • All counted tests used 2025 versions (Claude 3.7 / Opus 4 / Sonnet 4.5 era); Research now runs on Claude Opus 5.5, which has not been independently tested.
  • DeepResearchEval labels the system "Claude-Sonnet-4.5" although its collection dates precede that model's release.
  • Mind2Web 2 has Amazon AGI co-authors; Amazon is an investor in Anthropic.
  • LiveResearchBench, ReportBench, DeepResearch Bench II and Rao et al. tested Claude models via the API, not the Research feature; not counted.
  • The overall score is exactly 57.5; exact .5 rounds up (Decision 0010).
