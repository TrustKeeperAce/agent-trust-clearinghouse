Summary

Manus is a commercial general-purpose AI agent used for research reports and multi-step digital tasks. Meta agreed to acquire it in December 2025; Chinese regulators ordered the deal unwound in 2026, some user data was deleted in August 2026 to comply with regulatory requirements, and Manus resumed independent operation in September 2026. Manus 2.0 (28 September 2026, new architecture) has not been independently tested, so no overall score is given. Independent tests of 2025 versions are listed as historical context only. Insufficient evidence. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Scope

This entry covers Manus as currently offered (Manus 2.0). Independent tests of 2025 versions are listed below as historical context only and are not counted, because the vendor describes Manus 2.0 as a new architecture rather than a version update.

Uncertainties

  • All independent tests found used 2025 versions; Manus 2.0 is a new architecture per the vendor and is untested.
  • Vendor-reported GAIA scores and Manus 2.0 efficiency figures are not used.
  • The two dental studies test diagnostic question answering rather than agentic tasks.
  • CAP has a competitor tie (one author interned at FellouAI, an evaluated system).
  • Manus 2.0 Cascade efficiency figures (23.2% fewer tokens, 28.2% less time, 32% lower cost; one vendor configuration) are not used.
  • Cue agents (own email, phone number, wallet; invite-only early access) have not been independently tested.
