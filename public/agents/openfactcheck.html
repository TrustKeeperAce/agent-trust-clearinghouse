Summary

OpenFactCheck is an open-source framework from MBZUAI researchers for building configurable fact-checkers, measuring how factual language models are, and benchmarking fact-checkers. Its fact-checking mostly reuses parts of earlier systems (FacTool, Factcheck-GPT). No independent evaluation that ran it was found; the only figures come from its developers' EMNLP 2024 demo and COLING 2025 papers. It is actively maintained (2.0 alpha releases in 2026). No CVEs or security advisories were found. Insufficient evidence for an overall score. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Scope and version note

This entry covers the open-source OpenFactCheck library (PyPI openfactcheck) up to 1.1.2, run with models and retrievers chosen by the user. Its fact-checking largely reuses components from earlier systems (FacTool, Factcheck-GPT), so results depend on the configuration. The 0.x to 1.1.2 releases are treated as continuous; the 2.0 alpha series (2026) has not been assessed. The hosted web app is not covered; it did not respond on 17 October 2026.
