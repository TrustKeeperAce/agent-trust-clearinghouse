Summary

Elicit is a commercial AI research assistant that searches academic papers and supports systematic-review steps (screening and data extraction). Its literature search is weak. In independent tests, mostly in health research, Elicit searches found about 18–40% of the studies identified by traditional systematic searches. Its data extraction agreed with human reviewers about 77–92% of the time on the variables tested, with some errors and made-up details, and screening caught 71–78% of relevant studies in a 2026 preprint. Researchers concluded that Elicit can assist but not replace human reviewers. Most tests used paid tiers between 2023 and 2026. Overall score 68. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Scope

This entry covers Elicit, all tiers, tested mainly on paid tiers (Plus, Pro and the Review/Systematic Review workflow). The free Basic tier was not tested separately. Elicit has changed often (a move from credits to subscriptions in July 2024, high-accuracy extraction by default from May 2025, and a Systematic Review workflow); tests from 2023 to 2026 are counted because the product is continuous. The results differ by task: searching is weak, screening is moderate and data extraction is stronger, so the overall score does not describe fitness for any single task.

Privacy and disputes

No lawsuit, regulatory action, security advisory or reported data incident was found. Elicit states that uploaded PDFs stay private to the user’s account, that it does not train on enterprise data, and that it holds a SOC 2 Type II report. These are vendor statements only; no independent public audit was found.

Uncertainties

  • Most tests used paid tiers (Plus, Pro, Review/Systematic Review workflow); the free Basic tier was not tested separately.
  • Elicit changed often (credits to subscription in July 2024; high-accuracy default from May 2025; Systematic Review workflow); the oldest study used April 2023 searches.
  • Two studies (Hilkenmeier et al., O'Rourke et al.) are listed but not counted: abstract only, not fully reviewed (full text paywalled).
  • Lagisz et al. used temporary free accounts provided by Elicit (no other Elicit involvement, per the authors); counted and labelled partly independent.
  • Studies are small and mostly in health sciences; results differ by task (search, screening, extraction).
  • The vendor's own evaluation (93.6% recall, 94–99% extraction accuracy) is not used.
  • The overall score is exactly 67.5; exact .5 rounds up (Decision 0010).

Sources

Independent performance sources (counted)

  • Lau et al. (Cochrane Evidence Synthesis and Methods 2025; York): Elicit Pro Review mode across four evidence syntheses; search sensitivity 39.5% (25.5–69.2%) vs 94.5%, precision 41.8% vs 7.55% — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Bernard et al. (BMC Medical Research Methodology 2025): searches in April 2023; found 17.6% of an umbrella review's included studies (plus 3 new); three identical searches returned 246, 169 and 172 results; its summary cited the review protocol — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Andersen et al. (Cochrane Evidence Synthesis and Methods 2025): extraction from 30 articles in July 2024, high-accuracy mode; precision, recall and F1 92%; review-specific variables 77%; 4 confabulations — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Bianchi et al. (Cochrane Evidence Synthesis and Methods 2025; Eastern Switzerland): 20 RCTs extracted Aug–Sep 2024; 20.7% equal, 45.7% partially equal, 29.3% more, 4.3% deviating; intervention effects 95% only partially equal — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Lagisz et al. (Research Synthesis Methods 2026; UNSW): 7 reviews, extraction tests in 2025; 70 of 90 prompts above 87% during development, lower on new articles; 90% value agreement across accounts (quotes 46%); 77% value match in high-accuracy mode. Partly independent: temporary free Plus access from Elicit (no other involvement, per authors) — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Vyas et al. (Acta Dermato-Venereologica 2026; Cardiff): 1 of 59 search results met inclusion criteria; extraction from 24 articles had 13 incorrect results; wrong JADAD inputs in 5 of 24; study location wrong in 5 of 24 — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
  • Wei et al. (medRxiv preprint, Oct 2026; CAMH/Toronto): Pro tier, Dec 2025–May 2026; search found 158 of 5,285 studies; abstract screening sensitivity 0.71, full-text 0.78, precision 0.38; extraction 85% helpful, 79% fully accurate — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17

Abstract only, not fully reviewed (not counted)

  • O'Rourke et al. (Western Journal of Nursing Research 2026): reference lists for 4 reviews; Elicit matched 3.7% of human references on average (Consensus 21.3%). Abstract only, not fully reviewed; not counted — independent other, accessed 2026-10-17
