Summary

Pactum is a commercial procurement-negotiation agent. Public evidence consists mainly of vendor materials, a 2022 Harvard Business Review case study co-written by Walmart executives and university researchers (64% supplier agreement in a pilot), and a 2026 master's thesis analysing 148 negotiations at one customer (64.9% agreement; about half of agreements included a discount, with a median discount of zero; suppliers rated the process more favourably than the outcome). We found fewer than three independent evaluations, so no overall score is given. Insufficient evidence. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Status: Insufficient evidence — no overall score is given (fewer than three independent performance sources). Independent performance sources: 1 (partly independent).
