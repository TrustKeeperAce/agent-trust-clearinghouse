Summary

OpenAI Deep Research is a commercial ChatGPT feature that writes cited research reports. In independent evaluations (DeepResearch Bench, ICLR 2026; Mind2Web 2, NeurIPS 2025; ReportBench; a citation-URL study; TTD-DR preprint), it ranked among the stronger systems tested on report quality and task success, while a share of its citations did not match their sources or did not resolve. Most tests used 2025 versions; the underlying model has changed since. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Version note

As of 2026-10-17: OpenAI updated deep research in February 2026 and removed the legacy mode on 26 March 2026; the counted tests used 2025 versions; the current version has not been independently tested. Sources: OpenAI release notes (archived 21 March 2026); OpenAI Help Center.

Security and disputes

No formal disputes specific to this feature were found. In September 2025 Radware disclosed ShadowLeak, a zero-click indirect prompt-injection issue: a crafted email could make deep research send Gmail data to an outside address when the Gmail connector was enabled. It was reported to OpenAI in June 2025 and fixed in August 2025.
