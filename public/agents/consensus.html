Summary

Consensus is a commercial AI search engine that answers research questions from scholarly papers. In six independent peer-reviewed tests (2024–2026, mostly health sciences), it found 9 of 23 eligible trials in a systematic-search test, gave no fully correct answer sets about retracted trials and included retracted studies without warning in 40% of topic overviews, agreed with expert evidence on 72% of bariatric-surgery statements, and received the highest expert quality ratings in an endodontics comparison. Overall score 62. Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification.

Version note

Consensus has been offered continuously, with features added over time: Deep Search (July 2025), and in 2026 a Research Agent, a larger corpus, citation grounding and a public API (vendor summary). The counted tests ran from 2024 to January 2026 and are counted because the product is continuous (as in Decision 0017). This entry covers all modes; the Deep Search and Research Agent modes were not separately tested, and the 2026 features postdate all tests.

Disputes

No lawsuit, regulatory action, data breach or security advisory was found. Consensus states that it does not train on user data and logs only anonymized query text; these are vendor statements, not independently verified.

Uncertainties

  • Tests mostly covered standard or Pro search; Deep Search and Research Agent not separately tested.
  • Citation Grounding (2026) postdates all tests.
  • Samples are small and mostly in health sciences; several tests used the free tier.
  • The PRS Global Open study is qualitative (one question, no accuracy grading).
  • A retracted CJILS comparison was excluded.

Independent performance sources (counted)

  • Karakoyunlu et al. (BMC Oral Health 2026): Consensus Pro, Nov 2025; highest mDISCERN, GQS and completeness scores among four chatbots, longest response times — independent performance, accessed 2026-10-17
