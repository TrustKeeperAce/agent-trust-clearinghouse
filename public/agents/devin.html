Summary

Devin is a proprietary cloud coding agent from Cognition AI that works in its own virtual machine and opens pull requests. Twelve independent sources that tested it were found (five peer-reviewed): eleven field studies of its public GitHub pull requests in 2024-2025 and an industry evaluation. Pull-request acceptance ranged from 30% to 68.0% and was usually among the lowest of the five agents compared, although it rose over time; an informal month-long review (not counted) reported 3 successes in 20 tasks. Its March 2024 launch demo was disputed, a 2024 access issue was fixed, and 2025 security research showed prompt-injection attacks that leaked data. No legal or regulatory action was found. Scope

The Devin cloud agent from its March 2024 announcement through Devin 2.x (Devin 2.0 released April 2025). No counted study states which Devin version it used. Windsurf (acquired by Cognition in July 2025), DeepWiki and other Cognition products are not covered. Results depend heavily on the language model and task.

How sources were counted

Cognition's own reports, including the self-reported 13.86% SWE-bench result from March 2024, are not counted. No counted study has Cognition authors. Pull-request field studies count; most use the same AIDev dataset, so they are not independent samples. Commentary on the 2024 launch demo, security research and informal reviews (such as the Answer.AI month-long review) are listed as other sources.

Security and disputes

Devin runs commands and a browser in its own cloud machine. CVE-2024-56083 (CVSS 8.1) reported that a session's live-share URL could give write access to code; it was fixed on 12 December 2024. A security researcher reported prompt-injection attacks to Cognition in April 2025 and published them in August 2025, saying follow-up questions went unanswered. The March 2024 launch video of an Upwork job was disputed by a software engineer and by the client; Cognition said Devin is often inefficient and makes mistakes. No legal or regulatory action was found.

Uncertainties

  • Most counted sources share the AIDev pull-request data from 2024-2025; Devin 2.x and later models may behave differently.
  • No counted study states the Devin version.
  • Acceptance rates depend on repository, task type and reviewer behaviour.
  • The 2025 security findings were not confirmed as fixed in the sources found.
  • Citation check incomplete — recheck later (Semantic Scholar was rate-limited; OpenAlex was used).

Sources

Independent performance sources (counted)

  • Comparing AI Coding Agents (Trieste, KCL, UCL; MSR 2026, arXiv 2602.08915): Devin pull requests accepted at 68.0% in the common May-July 2025 window (Codex 79.9%); Devin was the only agent whose acceptance rose steadily (+0.77 points per week over 32 weeks). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Where Do AI Coding Agents Fail? (Drexel, Missouri S&T; MSR 2026): Devin merge rate 53.76% (Codex highest; Copilot 43.04%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Why Are AI Agent-Involved Pull Requests (Fix-Related) Remain Unmerged? (Saskatchewan; MSR 2026, arXiv 2602.00164): fix-related Devin pull requests merged at 42.9% (Codex 81.6%, Copilot 42.4%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • When AI Agents Touch CI/CD Configurations (Trent; MSR 2026, arXiv 2601.17413): Devin changed CI/CD files most often of five agents (4.83% of changes; Codex 2.01%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Security in the Age of AI Teammates (Notre Dame; Information and Software Technology 2026, arXiv 2601.00477): security-related Devin pull requests merged at 52.12% (Codex 86.59%, Copilot 49.60%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • The Rise of AI Teammates in SE 3.0 (Queen's University; AIDev dataset; preprint): Devin pull requests accepted at 49% (Codex 64%, Copilot 35%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Why Agentic-PRs Get Rejected (Kyushu, Waterloo; preprint): Devin acceptance 55.5%; 32.1% of rejected Devin pull requests were closed for inactivity, consistent with Devin auto-closing inactive pull requests. Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • How Do AI Coding Agents Contribute to Software Development? (Polytechnique Montréal; preprint Jul 2026): estimated merge probability 43.0% for Devin, the lowest of five agents (Claude Code 84.3%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Merged, Not Measured (preprint Sep 2026): Devin performance-fix pull requests accepted at 30%, the lowest of six agents; acceptance was near zero in January-April 2025, when almost all fixes in the set were Devin's. Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Not All Agents Are Equal (Texas Tech; preprint Sep 2026): merged Devin pull requests were reverted more often than human ones (14.5% vs 11.5%, odds ratio 1.31). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Trust but Verify? (UTSA; preprint Jul 2026): 39.7% of Devin pull requests carried a security smell (corpus average 38.9%). Field study — accessed 2026-10-17
  • Anthar Study (Deccan AI; industry report): 43 real pull-request tasks; Devin had the highest correctness on Python tasks (95.3% correlation with reference patches); possible context leakage between tasks was observed in IDE-based agents including Devin. Disclosure: Deccan AI sells evaluation data — accessed 2026-10-17
