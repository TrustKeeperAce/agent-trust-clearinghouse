Summary

Kimi Researcher is Moonshot AI's deep-research agent, offered as Deep Research in Kimi. Two independent evaluations were found. On DeepResearch Bench (mid-2025) its report quality was mid-tier, close to Claude Research and below OpenAI and Gemini deep research. On ICBCBench, a 2026 financial-research preprint, it ranked third among closed systems. Fewer than three independent sources were found, so no overall score is given. Version note

Kimi-Researcher was announced on 20 June 2025 and is offered as Deep Research in Kimi. Moonshot’s help page says Deep Research is powered by the Kimi-Researcher model. Kimi’s general models have since changed (K2.5, K2.6, K3), but no change of the Deep Research model has been announced, so the product is treated as continuous.

Disputes

On 20 April 2026 the Kimi app reportedly returned another user’s real résumé (name, phone number, email and work history) during an unrelated translation request. Staff reportedly described it first as an “AI hallucination” and then as a cross-user mix-up; Moonshot issued no public statement. This affects Kimi generally and was not reported as specific to Deep Research. Reports: OECD AI incident monitor, Phoenix Weekly (page unavailable as of 2026-10-17), Sina / Cover News, Seoul Economic Daily. No regulator action against Kimi was found.

Privacy note

Kimi is operated by Moonshot AI, a China-based company. Its privacy policy has not been reviewed for this entry, and its data handling and storage have not been independently verified.
