## Draft entry summary
Clay is a commercial product in the Data enrichment category. This entry is scoped to Claygent (Clay's AI web-research agent) and its enrichment features. Proposed status: **Insufficient evidence**, with no overall score. I found **0 independent performance sources** and 0 other independent sources. What exists is vendor material, partner marketing, practitioner posts and review aggregates.

## What happened
- **Vendor claims:**
  - GPT-5 raised Claygent research accuracy 8–17% on Clay's internal, unpublished web QA set.
  - In a Clay-run test of personal-email providers, 53% of lookups returned an email and 43% of 11,770 attempts were confirmed valid. That test covers marketplace providers, not Claygent.
- **Partner marketing:** an OpenAI customer story says Claygent cross-verifies data across providers and models.
- **Practitioner tests (anecdotal, small, self-published on LinkedIn):**
  - 10 rows: Argon found 10/10 answers; Neon 6/10 and Helium 5/10, with the answers they did give all accurate.
  - 1,000 tasks: Neon returned results for 788, but accuracy was not checked.
  - 100 rows graded with Clay itself as the "gold standard", so the comparison is circular.
- **Review aggregates (anecdotal):**
  - G2: 4.6/5 from 232 buyer reviews. G2's own task-based accuracy and policy-compliance evaluation lists Claygent as "Not evaluated".
  - Trustpilot: about 2.2/5 from roughly 9 reviews, seen in a snippet only; the page returned 403.
- **Vendor-stated limits:**
  - Per-plan action and data-credit allowances, plus optional auto top-ups.
  - Per-run budgets and output caps.
  - Minimum provider tiers when customers use their own API keys.
  - An API rate limit that returns 429 responses.
  - The DPA makes the customer the controller and Clay the processor, with the customer responsible for lawful processing.
- **Disputes:** no lawsuits, regulatory actions or data-protection complaints against Clay were found.

## Plain-English terms
- **Enrichment:** filling in missing data about companies or contacts.
- **Coverage vs accuracy:** whether an answer is returned at all, versus whether the answer is correct.
- **Circular grading:** scoring a tool against its own output.
- **Controller/processor:** under GDPR, who decides on the data versus who handles it on their behalf.
