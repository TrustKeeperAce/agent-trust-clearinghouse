## What the rules say
- Scores, grades, counts and the scoring rules are not touched by navigation links.
- Informational only: nothing may read as a ranking, recommendation or endorsement. Listing order on the site is not a ranking (methodology).
- "Last reviewed" changes only when a packet re-checks an entry's sources and scores; formatting and link changes do not change it.
- Every change to an entry page is recorded in the changelog and the update feeds.

## What changes (recommended set)
- **scripts/build_data.py:** a new build_related() step. It removes any existing related section and inserts a fresh one just before `</main>` on each entry page whose category has at least one other entry.
- **scripts/check_export.py:** a new check 0098. It re-derives the rule from agents.json and checks:
  - one section where the rule needs one and none on single-entry categories;
  - the section is last in `<main>`;
  - the links are exactly the other same-category entries, in alphabetical order, with no self-link;
  - every linked page exists;
  - the comparison links come from the comparison-page config;
  - no other links appear in the section;
  - no numbers, scores, grades or status words appear in it;
  - banned wording is absent;
  - the section matches the generator output;
  - llms-full.txt has no related sections.
- **41 entry pages** each gain the section. Clay and Pactum are unchanged.
- **data/changelog.yaml** gets one row (2026-10-23-0098, no entry changes). changelog.html, updates.json and updates.xml are regenerated with it.
- **Not changed:** agents/*.yaml, agents.json, agents.csv, schema.json, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, sitemap.xml, index.html, methodology.html, the comparison pages, the citing page, the stylesheet and data/page_only_links.yaml.

## Uncertainties
- A same-category list says nothing about how alike two products are. The Coding agent category mixes terminal tools, editor agents, cloud agents and an app builder. The note and the comparison-page caveats tell readers to read each scope.
- Visitors may still read list position as order of merit. The alphabetical rule, the stated note, and the absence of scores next to the links are meant to limit this.
- If an entry's category changes in a later packet, its list and the lists on the other pages in both categories change on the next build. Check 0098 fails until the pages are rebuilt.

## Plain-English terms
- **Category:** the one group each entry is filed under in the data, such as Coding agent or Research.
- **Comparison page:** the existing side-by-side pages, which show the entries' published figures together.
- **Generated:** written by the build script from the data each time the site is built, not typed by hand.

Informational only; not a guarantee, endorsement, or certification. Listing order is not a ranking.
